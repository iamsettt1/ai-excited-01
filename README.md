"The Leap": a colossal luminous teal whale breaches out of a night ocean, its whole body arcing through a star-filled indigo sky toward a huge golden-ringed planet, mouth slightly open and one eye visible at the peak of the leap, gold rim light along its back, a crown of spray bursting where its tail leaves the water and a trail of glowing droplets echoing its path. The image is drawn entirely by code: `main.py` is a single-file, standard-library-only Python 3 procedural renderer (seeded PRNG, distance-field whale, banded planet with a tilted ring, hand-rolled PNG encoder) that deterministically writes a 1024x1024 `entry.png`, byte-identical on every run, with no image assets and no network.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
