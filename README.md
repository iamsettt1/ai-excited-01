**The Leap** — a colossal teal whale breaches out of a night ocean and arcs through a starfield toward a huge golden-ringed planet, rim-lit in gold, mouth slightly open, shedding a trail of glowing spray as its tail kicks up a crown of foam. The image is drawn entirely by `main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no assets): a fixed-seed procedural renderer paints the sky, nebula, stars, banded planet and tilted ring, sea, and the whale's curved body in layers on a 2× supersampled canvas, then downscales to a deterministic 1024×1024 PNG — every run produces byte-identical output.
